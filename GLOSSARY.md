# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy courses and work through their lessons.

## Language

### Buying and refunding

**Purchase**:
A learner's one-time payment for a Course, which gives them access to it.
_Avoid_: Subscription, order

**Refund Request**:
A learner's request, with their reason, to get back the money paid for a Purchase. A Purchase can have only one, and its amount is fixed when it is made.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, reversal, chargeback

**Resolve**:
An Admin's final decision on a Refund Request: to **approve** it, which grants the Refund, or to **decline** it, with a note explaining why.
_Avoid_: Accept, reject, process

**Refund**:
The money returned to the learner when a Refund Request is approved. It is always the full refundable amount, and it ends the learner's access to the Course.
_Avoid_: Reversal, chargeback, cancellation, partial refund

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a Purchase during which a Refund Request can be made.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Refund Eligibility**:
The rules that decide whether a Refund Request can be made for a Purchase, such as the Refund Window and how much of the Course has been watched. It is judged when the request is made, not when it is resolved.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer
